FAQ

  • How do I delete something Ollie remembers? Go to Me → Memories, tap the item, and edit or delete it.
  • Ollie reaches out too often. Change the frequency, quiet hours and calls in Settings → How Ollie reaches you.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete account. All chats, memories and diaries are deleted permanently right away.
  • Will I lose my memories on a new phone? If you started as a guest, link an Apple, Google or Kakao account on the Me screen.
