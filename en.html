Ollie Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 (Version 2.0, complete revision)

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If there is any difference, the Korean version prevails. Read the Korean version.

In providing the mobile application Ollie — a cloud friend who remembers you (iOS and Android identifier: com.richjun.ollie; hereinafter "Ollie" or "this app"), Junhyeong Kim (hereinafter the "Developer") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws to protect users' personal information, and processes personal information as follows.

Ollie is not a medical service. Ollie is an AI conversation friend that listens to you and helps you take care of your mind. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. If you are in danger or feel you may harm yourself, please contact right away the Suicide Prevention Hotline 109 (24 hours), the Mental Health Crisis Hotline 1577-0199, or the emergency numbers 112 / 119 (these numbers apply in Korea).

Outside Korea, please call your local emergency number or find a free helpline at findahelpline.com.

1-2. Conversations and memories (generated while using the service)

  • Conversation content: messages sent by the user and Ollie's replies, and conversation summaries
  • Call records: text transcripts of voice calls, call summaries, call time and duration (the original audio is not stored)
  • Ollie's memories: short sentences Ollie remembers from conversations, calls and diary entries (e.g., "interview next Tuesday"), the numeric vectors used to retrieve them, and memory summaries
  • Diary: the text of diary entries left by voice or in writing, Ollie's comments, and emotions and inner weather
  • Inner weather check-ins: the weather (sunny to stormy) chosen for each date, and notes
  • Scheduled follow-ups: plans that Ollie has agreed to ask about again (e.g., "ask after the interview is over") and the history of messages sent
  • Usage: the number of conversations and call time per day (used to apply the free usage limit)
  • Safety signal records: the time at which a crisis signal was detected during a conversation and the risk level (see 1-5 below)

1-3. Processing of voice (important)

  • Voice calls: During a call, the user's voice is relayed in real time through Ollie's server to Google LLC's Gemini API (Live) and is used to generate Ollie's spoken replies. Call audio is not recorded or stored; only the text transcript is stored so that Ollie can remember the conversation.
  • Voice diary: The recording is created as a temporary file on the device, passed through Ollie's server, and converted to text by the Gemini API. Immediately after conversion, it is deleted from the device and from server memory. Recordings are not stored on the server.
  • The microphone operates only when the user answers or places a call, or presses the record button. It does not listen continuously in the background.

1-4. Notice on sensitive information

Conversations and diary entries you share with Ollie may include information about your state of mind, health and other sensitive information under Article 23 of the Personal Information Protection Act that you choose to talk about. Ollie does not ask for such information as a purpose of collection, but because of the nature of the service it is processed. Therefore, we process it only after obtaining separate consent when the app is first launched, and we apply the enhanced protection measures in Section 5 below. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app settings (if you withdraw consent, you will not be able to use the conversation features).

1-5. Crisis signal detection

For the safety of users, conversations and calls are automatically monitored for crisis signals (such as expressions related to self-harm or suicide). When a signal is detected, Ollie shows information about crisis hotlines, and only the time of detection and the risk level are recorded. This determination is made automatically, and the Developer does not view the content of conversations or notify any outside organization.

1-6. Push notifications, payments and device information

  • Push: device push token, app version, operating system — only if you allow notifications
  • Payment (Ollie Plus): subscription product, expiration date, renewal status, and the transaction identifier issued by the payment platform — payment method information such as card numbers is processed by Apple and Google, and Ollie does not receive it.
  • Stored only on the device: sound effects, font, theme and language settings, and login tokens (in the device's secure storage)

1-7. Information we do not collect

  • Location information, contacts, photo/media library, text messages
  • Advertising identifiers and behavioral information for personalized advertising — Ollie has no advertising.

5. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

  • Content encryption: The content of conversations, call transcripts, memories and diary entries is stored in the server database encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and backup files are also kept as ciphertext.
  • Encryption in transit: All communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS/WSS (TLS).
  • Access control: Every request is processed only with the user's own login token, and login tokens have a short validity period and reuse detection (automatically revoked if stolen). Conversation content and tokens are not written to operational logs.
  • Data minimization: Original audio is not stored, and only the information necessary for the service is processed.
